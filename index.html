Questions

Common questions

Do I have to pay for the demo?

No. The demo is free, and you're under no obligation to go ahead with it.

How much does it cost if I go ahead?

Prices are listed on the pricing page. You pay 50% once you approve the demo and the other 50% before the site goes live.

Who owns the website and domain?

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and you keep the site once it's paid for.

Can I change things after it's live?

Yes. Small updates are covered by the optional monthly care plan, or you can pay for changes when you need them. See services.

Can you build an online shop or booking app?

Online shops with payments, logins and custom apps are outside what I offer. A simple booking form is available as an add-on. Ask me anyway and I'll tell you honestly if it's a fit.
